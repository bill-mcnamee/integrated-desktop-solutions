# Integrated Desktop Solutions, Inc.

**Technology, digital media, and creative ventures since 1990.**

## About

Integrated Desktop Solutions, Inc. (IDS, Inc.) is the legal business entity and organizational umbrella for the professional consulting and independent creative ventures of Bill McNamee.

The business began in 1990, when Bill McNamee established Echo Basin Systems Consulting in Mancos, Colorado as a part-time consultancy providing specialized database and computer-aided design services to Cultural Resource Management firms throughout the Four Corners region of the American Southwest. This work grew directly from Bill McNamee's academic background in archaeology and combined that experience with an emerging career in information technology.

Following a relocation to Maryland in 1993, Echo Basin Systems Consulting became Integrated Desktop Solutions (IDS). The business continued its database and CAD work while expanding into more traditional information technology services, including technology purchasing, hardware and software installation, training, backup services, and technical consulting.

In 1998, Integrated Desktop Solutions incorporated as Integrated Desktop Solutions, Inc. Over the following decades, IDS, Inc. expanded beyond traditional information technology into photography, digital media, web design, desktop publishing, virtual content creation, creative workflows, and independent publishing.

Today, IDS, Inc. remains the legal business entity and company of record for Bill McNamee's consulting and creative work, connecting a professional history spanning technology, digital media, photography, writing, and publishing.

## Highlights

Integrated Desktop Solutions, Inc. (IDS, Inc.) traces its history through decades of technology, consulting, digital media, and creative work.

### 1990 | Echo Basin Systems Consulting

Echo Basin Systems Consulting was established in Mancos, Colorado as a part-time consultancy providing specialized database and CAD services to Cultural Resource Management firms throughout the Four Corners region. The work brought together Bill McNamee's academic and professional background in archaeology with his emerging career in information technology.

### 1993 | Integrated Desktop Solutions

Following a relocation to Maryland, Echo Basin Systems Consulting became Integrated Desktop Solutions (IDS). The business continued its database and CAD work while expanding into broader information technology consulting and support.

### 1997 | Integrated Desktop Solutions Goes Online

Integrated Desktop Solutions acquired the webids.com domain and established its first website, creating an online presence for the growing consulting business.

### 1998 | Integrated Desktop Solutions, Inc.

Integrated Desktop Solutions incorporated as Integrated Desktop Solutions, Inc., establishing the legal business entity that continues today.

### 1999 | Creative Services

Integrated Desktop Solutions, Inc. expanded beyond traditional information technology consulting with the addition of photography and desktop publishing services, beginning a broader evolution into digital media and creative work.

### 2004 | Full-Time Operations

Integrated Desktop Solutions, Inc. became a full-time technology and digital media consulting business. From 2004 through 2010, the company provided services spanning IT support, workflow design, web design, photography, digital media, and web hosting.

### 2004 | Light Imaging Studios

Light Imaging Studios was established under the IDS, Inc. umbrella as a dedicated identity for photography and imaging services. The venture operated through lightimagingstudios.com before its services were consolidated under BillMcNamee.com.

### 2005 | BillMcNamee.com

BillMcNamee.com was acquired in 2005, establishing a unified public-facing identity for Integrated Desktop Solutions, Inc. and Light Imaging Studios services. The site later evolved into the primary home for Bill McNamee's photography, writing, and creative work.

### 2006 | Raw Addiction Design & Development

Raw Addiction Design & Development (RADD) was established within Linden Lab's Second Life as a virtual business operated through Bill McNamee's business avatar, Bill Tandino. RADD created digital content, participated in virtual real estate, and developed simulator environments for real-world businesses seeking an in-world presence.

### 2010 | Transition to Part-Time Operations

In response to changing economic conditions and personal priorities, IDS, Inc. transitioned from full-time to part-time operations in 2010. The change preserved the company while allowing its consulting, digital media, and creative work to continue alongside Bill McNamee's full-time professional career.

### 2011 | Kerrsplat.com

The kerrsplat.com domain was acquired in 2011 and developed as a creative platform showcasing original writing by Bill McNamee along with work from other writers and artists.

### 2015 | Fotos For Change

Fotos For Change was established under the IDS, Inc. umbrella to document, photograph, and publish stories involving various social, urban, and environmental causes throughout the Mid-Atlantic region. The project became dormant after the corporate sponsorship required to pursue its original nonprofit model could not be secured.

### 2016 | Off Season: Cape May, New Jersey

The first edition of *Off Season: Cape May, New Jersey* was published through Blurb, bringing together photography, book design, and independent publishing.

### 2017 | Kerrsplat Consolidation

The original Kerrsplat website was closed, with Bill McNamee's original writing and creative content consolidated under BillMcNamee.com.

### 2025 | The Art of the Create

BillMcNamee.com was rebranded as **Bill McNamee | The Art of the Create**, bringing photography, writing, technology, creative workflows, and independent projects together under a unified creative identity.

### 2025 | Assistive & Generative AI

Assistive and generative AI became an increasingly integrated part of IDS, Inc. professional and creative workflows, supporting writing, editorial development, visual content, workflow design, and experimentation with emerging technologies.

### 2026 | Kerrsplat Publishing

Kerrsplat Publishing was established as an independent publishing imprint through Bowker, formalizing publishing activities under the IDS, Inc. umbrella.
